Data Deletion Instructions

How to revoke Facebook access and ask the app operator to delete retained information.

Last updated: November 8, 2026

The Facebook publishing integration is used for authorized Facebook Page posting. You may revoke its Meta access and request deletion of information retained by the App operator.

Option A: Remove access through Facebook

  1. Sign in to the Facebook account with which you authorized the integration.
  2. Open Settings & privacy → Settings, then locate Business Integrations or Apps and Websites (the location may vary by account).
  3. Find the relevant publishing integration and choose Remove or the available option to revoke its access.

Revoking access stops the integration from using that authorization for future actions. It does not necessarily delete copies of information already retained by the operator or posts already published to a Page.

Option B: Request deletion of stored App data

  1. Email waqarahmedch@hotmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line Facebook App Data Deletion Request.
  3. Identify the Facebook Page or integration concerned and the information you want removed, so the operator can locate relevant records. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or sensitive identity documents.
  4. The operator will review the request, delete applicable locally retained authorization data and records when appropriate, and reply with confirmation or an explanation of any information that must be retained for legal or security reasons.

Posts already published

To remove material previously published to a Facebook Page, an authorized Page administrator should remove that material directly using Facebook's Page management tools. Removal of App access or locally retained data does not automatically erase published posts.

Questions
