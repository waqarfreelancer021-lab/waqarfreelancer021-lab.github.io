Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

1. Operator and scope

Waqar operates Waqar TikTok Account Manager, a private integration intended for four owner-controlled TikTok accounts. This policy applies to the TikTok integration and its website. It does not replace the separate policy for the existing YouTube uploader.

Privacy contact: drwaqaruog@gmail.com.

2. Current setup status

TikTok account authorization is currently disabled. These static pages do not collect TikTok authorization codes, access tokens, refresh tokens, or TikTok passwords. Before account connections are enabled, this policy will be updated to identify the actual backend, storage providers, permissions, and retention practices.

3. Website visits and contact messages

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request information, when serving pages, under its own privacy statement.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use this information to respond to your request, provide support, and address privacy or deletion questions. Please do not email passwords, client secrets, or account tokens.

4. Intended TikTok integration

When enabled, the integration will ask each account owner to authorize access through TikTok. Depending on the permissions approved by the account owner and TikTok, the service may receive an account identifier, basic profile information, authorization codes, access tokens, refresh tokens, and token expiration details.

The purpose of that processing will be to connect the authorized accounts and perform only the features explicitly enabled by the operator. Publishing content will require a separate, explicit action or configuration by the operator.

5. Security requirements before activation

Before authorization is enabled, the backend must provide server-side code exchange, validation of OAuth state, restricted access to account data, encrypted token storage, and separate records for each authorized account. Client secrets and tokens must not appear in public website code, public repositories, browser storage, or public responses.

6. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising. Information may be processed by providers needed to host the website, deliver email, or operate the integration, and may be disclosed when required by applicable law. The providers used for the OAuth backend and token storage will be identified here before activation.

Provider processing may occur in countries outside your country of residence, subject to the provider's applicable terms and privacy practices.

7. Retention and deletion

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal obligations. Unneeded correspondence is deleted.

No TikTok account tokens are collected through these static pages. A specific account-data retention and deletion policy, including any backup retention, will be published before the account connection feature is enabled.

To request access, correction, or deletion of information you have provided, email drwaqaruog@gmail.com with the subject “TikTok Privacy Request.” We may need to verify your identity before acting. Do not include passwords or tokens.

8. Revoking TikTok access

After connecting an account, you may revoke the integration's access using TikTok's connected-app permissions settings. Revoking access and requesting deletion of stored records are separate actions; contact us if you also want stored data deleted.

9. Cookies and analytics

These static pages do not set application cookies or include advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Any necessary security cookies introduced for account authorization will be described before that feature is enabled.

10. Children

This private integration is intended for its adult operator. It is not directed to children.

11. Changes

This policy may change as the integration is developed. Updated versions will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
