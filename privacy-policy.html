Privacy Policy

How information is processed by this private Facebook publishing integration.

Effective date: November 8, 2026

1. About this integration

This Privacy Policy covers a private Facebook publishing integration (the “App”) operated for the purpose of posting content to Facebook Pages that its operator owns or is authorized to manage. This website provides legal information only; it does not offer public account registration.

2. Information the App processes

To authenticate and publish using Meta services, the App processes limited information as needed for its features. This may include:

  • Facebook Page identifiers, Page names, and the permissions granted to the App;
  • access tokens or other credentials supplied through an authorized Meta authentication process;
  • content selected for publication, including captions, images, videos, and publishing details;
  • technical information about API requests, successful publishing, or errors needed to operate and troubleshoot the integration.

The App is not intended to collect sensitive personal information from the public or access information from Facebook accounts and Pages that have not authorized it.

3. How information is used

Information is used to connect authorized Facebook Pages, submit content requested by the operator, check the status of publishing operations, maintain security, and resolve technical errors. It is not sold or used to build advertising profiles.

4. Sharing and third-party services

Information necessary for publishing is sent to Meta through its official APIs. Meta handles information under its own terms and privacy policies. Hosting, infrastructure, or automation providers used by the operator may also process limited technical information to perform these functions. The App does not sell personal data to third parties.

5. Retention and protection

The operator retains operational data only as long as needed to run the integration, satisfy a valid legal obligation, or investigate security and reliability issues. Authorization tokens should be protected from public disclosure and removed when access is no longer needed. No method of storage or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Access, revocation, and deletion

Authorized users can disconnect the App using Facebook's settings for Apps and Websites or Business Integrations, where available. To request deletion of information retained by the App operator, follow the Data Deletion Instructions.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised if the integration or its data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest published version.
